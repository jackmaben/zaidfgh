02 What we believe

Four commitments we hold to on every project

These are not values on a wall. They are the things clients tell us made the difference, and the things we will push back on you about if a project starts drifting away from them.

1Honesty

We will tell you when not to build it

Several times a year we talk a prospective client out of a project — because an off-the-shelf tool covers it, or because the problem is a process problem wearing a software costume. It costs us revenue and it is why people come back.

  • Free discovery call with a real recommendation attached, even if it is "don't"
  • Estimates that include the parts you would rather not hear about
2Seniority

No bait-and-switch on the team

The engineers in your kickoff call are the engineers who write the code. We do not staff projects with juniors after the contract is signed, and we do not run twelve engagements per person.

  • Named team, fixed for the length of the engagement
  • Direct access — your questions go to the person who can answer them
3Ownership

The code, the infrastructure and the knowledge are yours

Everything ships into your repositories and your cloud accounts from day one. No proprietary framework you cannot leave, no hosting arrangement that quietly becomes a subscription, no documentation gap that keeps you dependent.

  • Full IP transfer written into every contract
  • Handover includes architecture docs, runbooks and a working session with your team
4Evidence

We measure whether it worked

Every project starts with a number we are trying to move and ends with a review of whether it moved. When it does not, we say so and work out why before asking for more budget.

  • Problem statement agreed in writing before the build starts
  • Post-launch review at 30–90 days against that same statement
03 Where we work

Two studios, one team, overlapping hours

Our engineering base is in Ahmedabad and our client-facing team works from San Francisco. In practice that means four to five hours of daily overlap with US business hours, and full working coverage for European clients.

Want to know if we are a fit?

A discovery call costs you nothing and forty-five minutes. You will leave with a plain assessment of your problem — and a recommendation, whether or not it involves us.
